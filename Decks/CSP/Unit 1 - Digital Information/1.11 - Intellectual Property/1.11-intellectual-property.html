A Real Story

Thing #17240

I designed a box and posted it on the website Thingiverse. Anyone can download the file and use a laser to cut out a box any size. The file automatically calculates everything.

Since posting the design, I’ve seen these boxes on TV. Companies use them to demonstrate their $20,000 lasers.

I’ve seen these boxes for sale on the internet. There have been over 100,000 downloads of the box file.

Through all this, I’ve made $40 in “tips”. How would you feel?
