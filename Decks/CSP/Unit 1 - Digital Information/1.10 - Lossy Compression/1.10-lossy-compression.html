Wrap Up

Lossy Compression

Lossy compression: a process for reducing the number of bits needed to represent something in which some information is lost or thrown away. This process is not reversible.

01101100 01101111
01110011 01110011
01101100 01100101
01110011 01110011
↓ compress
01101100 1 … 0111
↓ decompress
01101100 01101111
01110011 01100011
01101000 01100101
01110001 01110011
≠ not identical to the original
