Avoid Bias in Your Design

Bias in What You Write

Collaboration is a big part of app development. Your team’s different experiences help you catch bias that either partner might miss on their own.

For Example

A team member writes the phrase “All teenagers work after school to earn spending money” because that’s their own experience: they have a job. Their partner doesn’t work after school, and points out the bias in that statement.

Avoid Bias in Your Design

Bias in What You Design

Bias can show up in more than just words. It can also hide in the choices you make about how your app looks and feels.

Another Example

A team wants to build an app that encourages girls to sign up for a summer camp. One member assumes the background should be pink, because the app is aimed at girls. A girl on the team points out that she doesn’t like pink, and that choice could represent bias too.

As you design your app, keep an eye out for bias, and lean on your partner to help you catch what you might miss.

Looking Forward

Looking Forward

In the lessons ahead, you’ll continue to plan and build your app. What you’re doing here is following a development process, similar to what real developers use when they create their own apps.

A development process breaks a big project into small pieces, and you’ve already completed the first few today. Different processes combine investigating, designing, prototyping, and testing in different orders, some very structured and some more exploratory. As you continue through this project, think about how each step helps organize your work.
