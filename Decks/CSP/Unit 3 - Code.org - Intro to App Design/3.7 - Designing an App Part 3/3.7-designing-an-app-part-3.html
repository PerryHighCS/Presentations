How We Work Today

Pair Programming With Your Partner

To start, the partner on the left is the driver and the partner on the right is the navigator. Every few minutes I will ask you to switch roles.

Driver

Controls the keyboard and writes the code the pair has agreed on.

Navigator

Keeps their head in the game, watches what the driver is doing, helps them along, and takes notes on the process.

It is also the navigator’s job to pick the radio station.
