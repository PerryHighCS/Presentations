Week 1 · Setup

Scheduling — Week 1

You’re trying to make plans to see your classmates. You need to meet with every classmate once during the week, and you can only meet with one person each day.

  • If you are a group of 6, no days need to be crossed out.
  • If you are a group of 5, everyone crosses out Sunday.
  • If you are a group of 4, everyone crosses out Saturday and Sunday.
  • Choose a random day of the week when you are busy and cross it out.
