Concept · Structure

Inside the NPN Transistor

An NPN transistor is a thin slice of P-type silicon, the base, between two pieces of N-type silicon: the emitter and the collector.

Each boundary is a PN junction, so the transistor holds two diodes back to back: base to emitter, and base to collector.

Concept · Transistor Action

How a Small Current Controls a Large One

A small base current forward biases the base-emitter junction, and electrons pour from the emitter into the base.

The base is thin and lightly doped, so only a few electrons find a hole or leave through the base lead. That small flow is the base current.

Most electrons sweep straight through into the collector. That large flow is the collector current. Conventional current runs the other way, from collector to emitter, which is why the symbol's arrow points out of the emitter.

Concept · Current Gain

Current Gain, β

The current gain, β (beta), tells you how many times larger the collector current is than the base current. For a 2N3904, β is between about 100 and 300.

Current gainβ = IC ÷ IB
Emitter currentIE = IB + IC

Example: a transistor with β = 150 has a base current of 0.1 mA.

IC = β × IB = 150 × 0.1 mA = 15 mA
IE = IB + IC = 0.1 mA + 15 mA = 15.1 mA

Every electron that leaves through the base or the collector came in through the emitter, so the emitter always carries the most current.

Concept · The PNP Transistor

The PNP Mirror

The 2N3906 is the PNP twin of the 2N3904: a thin N-type base between two P-type regions. Every current and voltage is reversed, and the symbol's arrow points in.

NPN: sinks current

The transistor sits between the load and ground. Current flows into the base to turn it on, and the transistor sinks the load current to ground.

PNP: sources current

The transistor sits between the supply and the load. Current flows out of the base to turn it on, and the transistor sources current to the load.

Industrial sensors come in both types, NPN (sinking) and PNP (sourcing). You will wire both in a later lesson.

Wrap Up

What We Know, and What Comes Next

Transistor action

A small base current lets a collector current up to β times larger flow, with no moving parts.

Two kinds of switch

An NPN switch below the load sinks current. A PNP switch above the load sources it.
