# Gear Train Research & Report Project

## Introduction

Explore how different types of gear systems transmit motion and torque in unique ways. You will research one type of gear train, explain how it works, and describe where and why engineers use it.

You will work in pairs or groups of three. Each team will be assigned one gear type from the list below:

* **Bevel Gear & Crown Gear Train** – changes rotation direction (usually 90°)  
* **Hypoid Gear Train** – specialized direction-changing systems  
* **Worm Gear Drive** – very high reduction, prevents back-driving  
* **Planetary (Epicyclic) Gear Set** – multiple inputs/outputs, compact ratio control  
* **Rack and Pinion** – converts rotary motion to linear motion  
* **Differential Gear** – splits torque and allows wheels to rotate at different speeds

## Part 1 – Research and Understanding

Guiding Questions:

1. What motion does this gear train create? (rotary → rotary, rotary → linear, etc.)  
     
2. What makes it different from a simple spur gear train?  
     
3. What is its gear ratio like (typical range, reduction or increase)?  
     
4. How does it change direction, speed, or torque?  
     
5. What are its advantages and disadvantages?  
     
6. What real-world applications use this type of system? (machines, vehicles, tools, robots, etc.)

## Part 2 – Visual Communication

Create a labeled diagram or model of your gear system. You can either create a **clear** hand-drawn, labeled sketch showing gear motion and direction, or find **quality** illustrations or animations of the gear system.

Your diagram must include:

* Labels for each gear (Driver, Driven, Idler, etc.)  
* Arrows showing direction of rotation  
* Ratio (if known or approximate)

## Part 3 – Summary Report

Create a [Slides presentation](http://slides.new) that includes:

* Gear system name  
* Purpose and type of motion  
* Advantages / disadvantages  
* Typical gear ratio or reduction  
* One or two real-world uses  
* Your labeled diagram
