# The Pounce: Mechanical Marvels Presentation

## Objective

Your invention is built. Now make the investors pounce\! Pitch your VEX machine as an invention worth supporting. Explain what it does, how its mechanical linkages work, and why investors should choose your team.

## Presentation Components

1. **Title Page**  
* Include the name of your Team and your Team Number  
* Include the name of your machine  
* Include the names of your team members  
2. **The Problem and the Pitch (Standards 1.1, 16.1)**  
* Identify the problem your invention solves, the task it performs, or the experience it creates.  
* Explain who would use or enjoy your machine and why.  
* Keep it concise and persuasive. What is your “hook”?  
3. **The Mechanical Design (Standard 10.0)**  
* Identify and demonstrate two linkages your machine uses (e.g., belt \+ gear, gear \+ bar linkage).  
* Explain how each linkage changes motion (speed, torque, direction, or force).  
* Explain a relevant gear ratio, speed change, or mechanical advantage and how it affects performance, if applicable.  
4. **Engineering Process (Standards 3.4, 11.4, 16.2)**  
* Highlight your design iterations—what didn’t work and how you fixed it.  
* Include any calculations or measurements (e.g., RPM, gear ratio, or belt tension).  
5. **Testing and Results (Standard 16.6)**  
* Demonstrate your VEX machine in action.  
* Describe the tests you ran (speed, strength, repeatability).  
* Discuss improvements you’d make with more time or resources.  
6. **Safety and Professionalism (Standard 2.0)**  
* Identify any safety considerations you followed during construction.  
* Mention teamwork roles or project organization.  
7. **The Ask**  
* Tell the investors what additional funding, materials, or development could help your invention succeed.  
* End with a confident pitch: why should the investors pounce on your invention?

## Format and Rubric

* **5–7 minutes total** (including demo of the machine).  
* **All team members must speak.**  
* **Evaluation criteria:**  
  1. Technical accuracy and mechanical understanding  
  2. Creativity and effectiveness of design  
  3. Clarity of presentation and teamwork  
  4. Use of engineering vocabulary  
  5. Persuasiveness of pitch
